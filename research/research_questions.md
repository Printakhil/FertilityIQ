
# FertilityIQ — Research Questions

## Primary Research Objective

Explore associations between clinical, laboratory, and
embryological characteristics and recorded IVF implantation
outcomes using a public research dataset.

## Research Questions

RQ1: How are female age and AMH distributed in the dataset?

RQ2: What patterns exist in the recorded male fertility
parameters?

RQ3: How are oocyte maturity and embryo grade variables
distributed?

RQ4: Are clinical and embryological variables associated
with the recorded implantation outcome?

RQ5: Which variables show the strongest statistical
associations with the outcome?

RQ6: How do missing values and extreme observations
affect the analysis?

## Research Limitations

- Dataset provenance and representativeness require assessment.
- Patient-level identifiers have not yet been verified.
- Clinical units and variable definitions need confirmation.
- Association does not imply causation.
- Statistical findings are exploratory, not clinical guidance.
- Post-treatment variables must not be used as pre-transfer
  predictors.
